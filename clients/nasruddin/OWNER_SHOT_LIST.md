# Photo shot list for the owner

Please photograph the nursery's real plants and working areas. Send original camera files or uncropped exports, without social-media text, prices, filters or interface overlays. Keep copies of all originals. Confirm that the business has permission to use any identifiable person's image.

## One matching pair for the opening

1. **Wide row:** photograph a full row of shaped/topiary plants from a clear position, showing the nursery setting and the spacing between specimens. Take a horizontal image for desktop and a vertical image for phones. Include a person standing naturally beside the featured plant for scale; leave room around the edges for responsive cropping.
2. **Matching specimen:** photograph one clearly identifiable plant from the same row, from a similar direction. Include the whole plant and pot or root ball. Place a measuring tape beside it without obscuring the plant, and record the exact measured height and where the measurement starts and ends. Take both vertical and horizontal versions.
3. **Matching detail:** photograph the trunk, pruning, foliage and container or root ball of that exact specimen. Keep identifying tags visible if present. Record the plant name, botanical name if known, date, and which photos are the same specimen.

For all three, use even daylight where possible, hold the camera steady, avoid portrait-mode effects and leave natural colors intact. Do not digitally alter the plant's shape, density or apparent size. The tape is a physical scale reference; the website will show a measurement only after it is verified.

## Helpful supporting material for later sections

- A few additional wide rows showing real inventory and the people who tend it.
- Clear images of confirmed plant sizes and containers, photographed consistently for comparison.
- Real, approved images of handling or loading only where they accurately reflect the service offered.
- Any approved landscaping project images, with the project name, location, date and permission to publish.

Please confirm the WhatsApp number, business name, current stock details, photography permissions and any shipping or export capabilities separately. Photos alone do not establish current availability or international delivery.
